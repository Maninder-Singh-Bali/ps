What is included

32 named rooms/spaces, 54 wall spans, 26 hosted openings (including sliding glazing), 64 furniture proxies and 59 surface items. Open-plan boundaries are editing regions, not additional walls. Four bedroom wings, patios, formal and family living, dining/kitchen, rear courtyard/deck and service areas remain inspectable.

How to inspect

  1. Open the furnished residence and use Whole apartment to inspect the complete layout. Select rooms from Sections.
  2. Use Plan for wall joins, opening attachment and dimensions. Use Surfaces for finishes, decor, mounting and ceiling details. Save only affects your browser copy.
  3. Inspect the saved living-room camera in Cameras: eye 1.5 m, target 0.9 m, horizontal FOV 60°. A rebuilt camera image is unavailable publicly.
  4. Use Testing controls → Reset browser-local data to restore the Heritage copy. Other scenarios are kept separately. Reload preserves your saved edits.

Questions for the reviewer

  • Are rooms and open-plan connections coherent? Identify specific wall, opening or circulation issues with a room/object name.
  • Are furniture dimensions and clearances plausible? The formal sofa-to-table gap is approximately 0.44 m. Do not treat the placement checker as door-swing or accessibility certification.
  • Are surface hosts, item positions, rotations and mounting dimensions consistent with the plan?
  • Which corrections are essential before one real living-room image test? Separate architectural defects from style preferences.

Actual geometry versus browser simulation

The baseline 3D geometry was exported through the local production shared-scene builder. The public site has no backend. Architecture and surface geometry in 3D are static; edited plan geometry cannot be rebuilt here. Furniture proxies update through the browser renderer. Camera settings are editable, but the preview does not claim to regenerate camera images. Generation and output approval are unavailable for this residence. Do not use this mock transport as proof of production geometry rebuilds, review validation or inference.

Known limitations and unfinished work

  • Front entry steps: risers, elevations and landing connection unmodeled.
  • Pool basin, drainage and levels unresolved.
  • Fine jali carving reference-guided, not modeled.
  • Product materials are proxies; intended ivory sofa reference photo was gray.
  • Direct 3D object-drag persistence not conclusively verified in local testing.
  • Rapid clicks during asynchronous live 3D rebuild can be overridden.
  • The current tighter camera includes the main seating, table, console and arch/art; chair feet and pendant are partly cropped. The physical jali is outside this camera. No rendered image comparison is available.
  • Style matching and source tracing cannot be independently checked against excluded photographs. The original source remains in the private local project.

Verification already performed locally

Room context, furniture dimensions/save-reload, wall-mirror undo/redo, 2D/Elevation/live 3D opening, view modes and ceiling-drop persistence were checked against the real local backend. Geometry checks found valid joined rings and valid hosts/opening bands; these checks do not prove surveying accuracy. Direct 3D object-drag persistence remains inconclusive. No production image attempt has run.
